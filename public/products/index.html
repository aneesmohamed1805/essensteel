Steel wire products

Galvanised steel wire is used in fencing, cable armouring and nail manufacture. Mattress spring wire is formed into springs for bedding, while duct wire serves cable installation and construction applications.

Steel strands

Prestressed concrete strand is used in bridges, rail infrastructure and buildings. ACSR steel core wire and strand reinforce aluminium conductors used in overhead power transmission. Confirm the required construction and grade against the applicable data sheet.

Specialist wires

For cable armouring, fence, nail or agricultural wire, share the intended application and the processing or installation requirements with Essen Steel.
