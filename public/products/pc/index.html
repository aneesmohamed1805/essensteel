PC Strand

Prestressing strand for concrete structures such as bridges, rail infrastructure and buildings.

Where it is used

Prestressing strand for concrete structures such as bridges, rail infrastructure and buildings.

In a concrete viaduct, the strand is tensioned to introduce compression into the girders that span between piers.

What to specify

For prestressing orders, share the governing standard, strand designation, nominal diameter and required test documentation. Confirm the coil or pack requirements with your installation team.

Include your quantity, delivery destination and target date when requesting a quote. Identify any certificate, inspection requirement or material test report that forms part of the order.
