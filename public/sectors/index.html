Industries and Applications

Explore how Essen Steel wire and strand are used across infrastructure, construction, power, telecommunications, agriculture, fencing and manufacturing.

Infrastructure

In a concrete viaduct, prestressing strand helps the girders carry loads between piers. Tension in the strand puts the concrete into compression. The structural design governs the strand designation, dimensions and test requirements.

For staged civil works, include the quantity and delivery window for each stage. Identify any inspection and material traceability requirements before placing the order.

Construction

Precast beams and floor units can be prestressed before they leave the casting yard. In pre-tensioned production, strand is tensioned in the casting bed and transfers that force to the concrete after it has gained sufficient strength.

For precast or prestressing work, establish the specification with the design and installation teams. Tell us about any restrictions on pack dimensions, handling or storage at the plant or site.

Power Transmission

An overhead line conductor has to span the distance between towers while carrying electrical current. In ACSR construction, the aluminium wires carry the current and the steel core provides reinforcement. Essen supplies the steel core wire or strand.

Conductor manufacturers should identify the required core wire diameter, coating and mechanical properties. Where the specification calls for a stranded core, include the construction and applicable test documentation.

Telecommunications

A telecom duct route and the cable installed within it are separate parts of the system. Duct wire is used in cable installation, while armouring wire may form part of the cable itself. Identify which application your specification covers.

Identify whether the wire is for a duct installation or an armouring process. The intended function determines the specification and which wire family to discuss.

Agriculture and Fencing

Wire for outdoor fencing is formed into mesh or used in other fence components. The zinc coating helps protect the steel against corrosion. For agriculture and fencing orders, confirm the intended exposure, wire diameter and coating requirement.

For fence manufacture or agricultural installations, explain the exposure conditions and downstream process. This helps establish which coating, wire properties and pack format the order requires.

Manufacturing

On a forming line, guides and rollers feed steel wire into the next operation. The finished part may be a nail, a cable armouring component or another industrial product. The forming process determines what the wire needs to do.

Share how the wire is fed, formed or cut, and which dimensions and mechanical properties affect your process. Note any limits on coil size or pack weight.

Bedding

The individual coils in a mattress spring unit start as steel spring wire. Bedding manufacturers form that wire into coils, then assemble them into the support system specified for the mattress.

Include the spring design requirements and how the wire feeds into your forming equipment. Confirm the pack format and the properties your production team needs to check.
