Power Transmission

Steel core wire and strand reinforce the aluminium wires in ACSR overhead conductors. Include the conductor designation and the steel core construction in your enquiry.

Conductor manufacturers should identify the required core wire diameter, coating and mechanical properties. Where the specification calls for a stranded core, include the construction and applicable test documentation.
