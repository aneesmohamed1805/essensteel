Industries and Applications

Find the wire or strand used in your application.

Infrastructure and construction

Prestressing strand is specified for concrete structures such as bridges, rail infrastructure and buildings. The structural design establishes the grade and dimensions. Include the governing standard and test documentation in your enquiry.

Explore PC Strand

Power transmission

In an ACSR conductor, the steel core provides reinforcement for the surrounding aluminium wires. Share the conductor designation and core specification when enquiring about steel core wire or strand.

Explore ACSR Steel Core Wire and Strand

Bedding and manufacturing

Mattress manufacturers form steel spring wire into components for bedding assemblies. Wire diameter, required mechanical properties and pack format are useful starting points for an enquiry.

Explore Mattress Spring Wire

Agriculture, fencing and industrial applications

Galvanised and specialist wires serve fencing, agricultural and industrial uses, including nail manufacture and cable armouring. Confirm the intended use and coating requirements when selecting the wire.

Explore Galvanised Steel Wire

Telecommunications and cable installation

For duct and cable work, identify how the wire will be used and share the dimensions and installation requirements. For cable armouring enquiries, include the relevant wire and coating specification.

Explore Duct Wire
