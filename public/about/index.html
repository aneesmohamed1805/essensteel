About Essen Steel

Steel wire and strand manufacturer in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates.

What Essen manufactures

From its facility in Kizad, Abu Dhabi, Essen Steel manufactures steel wire and strand for infrastructure, power transmission, construction and industrial manufacturing. The range includes prestressed concrete strand, ACSR steel core wire and strand, galvanised wire and specialist wires.

What the products support

Prestressing strand is used in concrete structures such as bridges, rail infrastructure and buildings. Steel core wire and strand are used in ACSR conductors, while other wire products serve bedding, fencing, agriculture and cable applications.

How Essen works with buyers

Essen works with engineers, procurement teams and manufacturers to clarify material requirements before supply. The discussion can cover the applicable standard, product grade, quantity, test documentation, packaging and delivery destination.
