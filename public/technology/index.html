Manufacturing and Technology

Steel wire and strand production in Kizad, Abu Dhabi.

Wire drawing and stranding

Wire drawing reduces the material to the diameter required for the order. Where the product is a strand, individual wires are formed into the specified construction. The required dimensions and mechanical properties depend on the product and its application.

Finishing and galvanising

Finishing depends on the intended use. For galvanised products, the coating specification forms part of the order alongside the wire dimensions and mechanical requirements.

Testing and traceability

Inspection and testing connect the manufacturing process to the buyer’s specification. Agree the relevant dimensional, mechanical and coating checks, along with any test reports and material identification required for delivery.

Packaging and supply

Share how the material will be handled and processed at the destination. Agree the packaging, labelling, quantity and delivery requirements as part of the order.
