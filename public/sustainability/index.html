Responsible Manufacturing

Manufacturing practices and environmental information for procurement teams.

Energy and resource use

Energy use, material waste and process control are focus areas for responsible wire and strand manufacturing. Buyers who track these factors can specify the product, reporting boundary and period they need when requesting evidence.

Material selection and service conditions

The right grade, dimensions and coating depend on the application. Share the operating conditions and applicable standard so the material specification can be reviewed for the intended use.

Environmental documentation

For supplier assessments or tenders, include the exact environmental or energy documentation required and any applicable reporting format.
