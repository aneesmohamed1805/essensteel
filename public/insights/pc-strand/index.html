Understanding PC Strand

How prestressing strand is used in concrete construction.

How prestressing works

Prestressing puts the concrete into compression. PC strand carries the tensile force that is transferred to the concrete according to the structural design.

What to confirm before ordering

When ordering, confirm the required grade, nominal diameter, governing standard, packaging and documentation with your technical team.
