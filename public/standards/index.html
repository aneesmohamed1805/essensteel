Quality and Certifications

Discuss product requirements, inspection and documentation.

Quality assurance through production

Quality assurance covers incoming material inspection, checks during production and final verification against the specification. Agree any project-specific inspection or acceptance requirements when requesting a quote.

Mechanical, dimensional and coating checks

Relevant checks may include tensile strength, elongation and dimensions. For galvanised products, specify the coating requirement and the test method or reporting format your project needs.

Material traceability

When mill test reports are required, agree the batch or coil identification and the information to appear in the report. Match the material identification and report to the purchase specification at receipt.

Certification and approval documents

Ask for the current certificate and scope for the product or facility involved. Identify whether the project requires management-system certification, product certification or an authority approval.
